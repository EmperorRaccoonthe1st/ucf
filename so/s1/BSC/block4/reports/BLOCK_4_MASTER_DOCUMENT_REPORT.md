## 2. Ingested Curriculum Inventory

A total of 10 Canvas modules, 4 lecture slide presentations (144 slides), 43 examination practice problems, and 22 official vocabulary terms were fused into the Master Reference deliverables:

### A. Chapter 12: The Cell Cycle & Mitosis
* **Canvas Modules (3 Modules):**
  * `Chapter 12 Content Introduction_ BSC2010C-26Fall 0001.html`: DNA organization hierarchy.
  * `Chapter 12 DNA and chromosomes_ BSC2010C-26Fall 0001.html`: Circular prokaryotic vs. linear eukaryotic chromatin, histones, nucleosomes, and looped domains.
  * `Chapter 12 The Cell Cycle_ BSC2010C-26Fall 0001.html`: Interphase (G1, S, G2), 5 mitotic phases, cytokinesis (cleavage furrow vs. cell plate), and molecular checkpoints (G1, G2/MPF, M/spindle).
* **Instructor Lecture Slides:**
  * `Chapter 12_student pt 1.pptx` (23 slides): DNA review, eukaryotic chromosome anatomy, centromeres, telomeres, species chromosome numbers, and homologs.
  * `Chapter 12_student pt 2.pptx` (28 slides): The cell cycle clock, mitotic spindle, kinetochore mechanics, cyclin/Cdk/MPF kinetics, density-dependent inhibition, and cancer bypass.
* **Practice Materials & Vocabulary:**
  * 11 dedicated practice problems with complete rationales from `Chapters 12 and 13 Practice Questions.docx`.
  * Official glossary definitions for somatic cells, gametes, autosomes, sex chromosomes, homologs, diploid (2n), haploid (n), sister chromatids, centromere, kinetochore, centrosome, and metaphase plate.

### B. Chapter 13: Meiosis & Sexual Life Cycles
* **Canvas Modules (3 Modules):**
  * `Chapter 13 Content Introduction_ BSC2010C-26Fall 0001.html`: Transmission of hereditary traits and ploidy foundations.
  * `Chapter 13 Meiosis and Sexual Reproduction_ BSC2010C-26Fall 0001.html`: Asexual reproduction (clones) vs. sexual reproduction (gametic diversity).
  * `Chapter 13 The Process of Meiosis_ BSC2010C-26Fall 0001.html`: Reductional Meiosis I (Prophase I synapsis, chiasmata crossing over, Metaphase I independent assortment, Anaphase I homologous segregation) and Equational Meiosis II (sister chromatid separation).
* **Instructor Lecture Slides:**
  * `Chapter 13_student.pptx` (58 slides): Complete meiotic mechanics, 3 mechanisms of genetic variation (crossing over, independent assortment 2^n, random fertilization), nondisjunction in Meiosis I vs. Meiosis II, aneuploidy (Trisomy 13, 18, 21; Turner X0; Klinefelter XXY; Jacobs XYY), and structural chromosome mutations (deletion, duplication, inversion, translocation).
* **Practice Materials & Vocabulary:**
  * 12 dedicated practice problems with complete rationales from `Chapters 12 and 13 Practice Questions.docx`.
  * Official glossary definitions for synapsis, crossing over, chiasma, recombinant chromosome, law of independent assortment, nondisjunction, aneuploidy, monoploidy/monosomy, polyploidy/trisomy, and tetrad.

### C. Chapter 16: The Molecular Basis of Inheritance
* **Canvas Modules (4 Modules):**
  * `Chapter 16 Content Introduction_ BSC2010C-26Fall 0001.html`: Deoxyribose 1' to 5' carbon labeling, purines vs. pyrimidines, phosphodiester bonds, antiparallel 5'->3' and 3'->5' polarity, and Chargaff's rules.
  * `Chapter 16 DNA Structure and Replication Introduction_ BSC2010C-26Fall 0001.html`: Landmark discoveries (Hershey-Chase, Franklin, Watson & Crick) and the Meselson-Stahl semiconservative replication experiment.
  * `Chapter 16 DNA Replication_ BSC2010C-26Fall 0001.html`: Replisome machinery (helicase, SSBPs, topoisomerase, primase, DNA Pol III, continuous leading strand, discontinuous lagging strand Okazaki fragments, DNA Pol I, DNA ligase).
  * `Chapter 16 The role of Telomeres_ BSC2010C-26Fall 0001.html`: The lagging strand end-replication gap, repetitive TTAGGG non-coding caps, the Hayflick limit / senescence clock, and telomerase reverse transcriptase in germline and cancer cells.
* **Instructor Lecture Slides:**
  * `Chapter 16_student.pptx` (35 slides): Tetranucleotide hypothesis refutation, Marie Maynard Daly nuclear base research, Chargaff math problems, hydrogen bond thermodynamics (A=T with 2 H-bonds, G=C with 3 H-bonds), 5'->3' synthesis constraint, 3'->5' exonuclease proofreading, mismatch repair, nucleotide excision repair, and the 8 core enzymes master table.
* **Practice Materials:**
  * All 19 official practice questions from `Chapter 16 Practice Questions.docx` with complete answers and explanations.
