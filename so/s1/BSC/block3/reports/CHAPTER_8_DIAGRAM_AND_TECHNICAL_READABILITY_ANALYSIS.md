## 3. Technical Content Readability Analysis

### A. Thermodynamics & Bioenergetics
* **The Living Paradox:**
  * Organisms are open systems that maintain low local entropy by constantly releasing heat and metabolic waste, increasing the net entropy of the universe.
* **Chemical Energy:**
  * High-energy fuels (lipids, carbohydrates) store chemical potential energy in nonpolar C-C and C-H bonds with relatively equal electron sharing.
  * Oxidation shifts electrons toward electronegative oxygen, releasing free energy to synthesize ATP.

### B. Free Energy (Delta G) & Equilibrium
* **The Gibbs Equation:**
  * `Delta G = Delta H - T Delta S`.
  * Reactions with `Delta G < 0` are exergonic, release usable energy, and are thermodynamically spontaneous.
  * Reactions with `Delta G > 0` are endergonic, require free energy input, and are non-spontaneous.
* **Metabolic Equilibrium:**
  * At equilibrium, `Delta G = 0` and work capacity is zero. Because living systems require continuous metabolic work, a cell at chemical equilibrium is dead.

### C. ATP & Energetic Coupling
* **Triphosphate Tail Instability:**
  * Four adjacent negative charges on oxygen atoms produce intense electrostatic repulsion, creating high chemical potential energy.
  * Hydrolysis yields `Delta G = -7.3 kcal/mol` (standard) or `~ -13 kcal/mol` (cellular conditions).
* **Coupling Rule:**
  * Energetic coupling requires `Delta G_net = Delta G_1 + Delta G_2 < 0`.
  * Enzymes transfer high-energy phosphate groups directly to substrates, forming reactive phosphorylated intermediates.

### D. Enzyme Catalysis & Kinetics
* **Thermodynamic Neutrality:**
  * Enzymes lower activation energy (`E_A`) to accelerate reaction rates, but **cannot alter Delta G or equilibrium**.
* **Induced Fit:**
  * Substrate binding induces conformational changes in active-site R-groups, contorting bonds toward their transition-state geometry.
* **Inhibition Kinetics:**
  * *Competitive:* Inhibitor competes for active site; overcome by increasing `[S]` (Vmax unchanged, Km increased).
  * *Noncompetitive (Allosteric):* Inhibitor binds elsewhere; distorts active site geometry; cannot be overcome by `[S]` (Vmax decreased, Km unchanged).
