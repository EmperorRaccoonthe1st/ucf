## 3. Technical Content Readability Analysis

### A. Redox Mechanics & Energy Extraction
* **Electron Shuttling:**
  * Catabolic breakdown is a series of redox reactions where high-energy electrons are stripped from glucose by dehydrogenases and transferred to NAD+ and FAD.
  * In respiration, glucose is oxidized to CO2 and oxygen is reduced to H2O.
  * *Readability Status:* `[PASS]` Cleanly organized with mnemonic visual callouts on Page 1.

### B. Phosphorylation Mechanisms
* **Substrate-Level vs. Oxidative:**
  * Substrate-level phosphorylation uses a soluble kinase to transfer a phosphate from an organic substrate to ADP (Glycolysis & CAC; 4 net ATP).
  * Oxidative phosphorylation utilizes the electron transport chain and chemiosmosis via ATP Synthase (~26-28 ATP).
  * *Readability Status:* `[PASS]` Structured in 3-column comparative tables on Page 2.

### C. The Four Respiration Stages
* **1. Glycolysis (Cytosol):**
  * 1 Glucose (6C) -> 2 Pyruvate (3C) + 2 Net ATP + 2 NADH. Does not require O2.
* **2. Pyruvate Oxidation (Matrix):**
  * 2 Pyruvate (3C) -> 2 Acetyl-CoA (2C) + 2 CO2 + 2 NADH. Catalyzed by pyruvate dehydrogenase.
* **3. Citric Acid Cycle (Matrix):**
  * 2 Acetyl-CoA condense with oxaloacetate -> 4 CO2 + 6 NADH + 2 FADH2 + 2 ATP (per glucose).
* **4. Oxidative Phosphorylation (Inner Membrane / Cristae):**
  * Complexes I, III, IV pump H+ into intermembrane space; electrons flow to terminal acceptor O2; ATP Synthase converts proton-motive force into 26-28 ATP.
  * *Readability Status:* `[PASS]` Dedicated single-page modules across Pages 3 to 7.

### D. Anaerobic Fermentation Pathways
* **The NAD+ Regeneration Dilemma:**
  * In the absence of O2, the ETC halts. Fermentation transfers electrons from NADH to organic acceptors (pyruvate in lactic acid fermentation; acetaldehyde in alcohol fermentation) to regenerate NAD+ for glycolysis.
  * *Readability Status:* `[PASS]` Detailed on Page 8.

## 4. Active Learning & Examination Verification

### A. Original Active Learning Practice Problems (Pages 9 & 10)
* **Problem 1 (Glycolysis & Pyruvate Oxidation Accounting):**
  * Tracks 2 pyruvate, 2 net ATP, and 2 NADH from glycolysis, and 2 Acetyl-CoA, 2 CO2, and 2 NADH from pyruvate oxidation with specific destinations.
* **Problem 2 (Citric Acid Cycle Accounting):**
  * Solves yields per turn and per glucose (6 NADH, 2 FADH2, 2 ATP, 4 CO2).
* **Problem 3 (ETC & Chemiosmosis):**
  * Maps H+ pumping into intermembrane space, NADH vs. FADH2 pathways, and terminal O2 reduction.
* **Problem 4 (Cellular Poisons Matrix):**
  * Shikonin (glycolysis blocker -> glucose accumulation), Arsenic (pyruvate dehydrogenase inhibitor -> pyruvate buildup), Rotenone (Complex I blocker -> NADH accumulation), DNP (membrane uncoupler -> gradient collapse & heat release), Oligomycin (ATP synthase blocker -> H+ accumulation in intermembrane space).
