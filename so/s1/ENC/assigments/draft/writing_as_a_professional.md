# Prompt

Review the information in Chapter 6 of Technical and Professional Writing Genres,https://open.library.okstate.edu/technicalandprofessionalwriting/chapter/chapter-6/ before working on the assignment below.
Purpose

Professional writers must be able to communicate information efficiently, respond to workplace situations, and select genres that fit their audiences and purposes. This assignment asks you to demonstrate your ability to write both a professional email and a professional memo.

Rather than treating these genres as templates to fill in, you will make deliberate choices about organization, tone, content, and format based on a workplace situation. You will also explain why your choices are appropriate for the intended readers.
Scenario

You work as an assistant coordinator in an organization related to your intended career field. Your department is preparing for an upcoming event, project launch, client presentation, or other professional activity.

Your supervisor has asked you to help coordinate the work. You have discovered that a required component is incomplete, and the team needs to resolve the issue before the final deadline.

You must communicate with a colleague to request the missing information and communicate with your supervisor or department team about the situation, its implications, and the next steps.

You may adapt this scenario to your intended profession, provided both documents address the same situation and serve different communication purposes.
Document 1: Professional Email

Audience: A colleague responsible for providing the missing information.

Purpose: Request the necessary information and establish a clear timeline for completing the task.

Write a professional email that:

    Uses a specific, informative subject line.

    Opens with the purpose of the message.

    Provides sufficient context to explain the request.

    Clearly identifies what the recipient needs to provide.

    Includes a realistic deadline or next step.

    Maintains an appropriate, respectful professional tone.

    Uses appropriate email conventions, including a greeting and signature.

Your email should be concise while providing enough information for the recipient to act without unnecessary follow-up.
Document 2: Professional Memo

Audience: Your supervisor or relevant department team.

Purpose: Explain the status of the incomplete task and recommend an appropriate response.

Write a professional memo that:

    Includes the standard memo heading: To, From, Date, and Subject.

    Clearly identifies the issue and its relevance to the project.

    Provides the necessary background and current status.

    Explains potential consequences or challenges, where appropriate.

    Recommends a reasonable course of action or identifies next steps.

    Organizes information so readers can locate important details quickly.

    Maintains an objective, professional tone.

The memo should not simply repeat the email. It should address a different audience and serve a different purpose by documenting the situation and supporting a decision or plan of action.
Document 3: Rhetorical Rationale

Write a brief rationale of approximately 200–300 words explaining the choices you made in the two documents.

Address the following questions:

    How did the different audiences and purposes influence your writing choices?

    Why did you choose an email for one message and a memo for the other?

    How did you adjust the level of detail, organization, tone, or directness for each audience?

    What did you do to make each document clear, concise, and actionable?

    What was the most challenging rhetorical decision you made, and why?

Your rationale should explain the reasoning behind your choices rather than merely list the features you included.
Submission Requirements

Submit one document containing:

    Professional Email

    Professional Memo

    Rhetorical Rationale

Use clear headings to separate the three sections. The email and memo should be formatted as workplace documents rather than traditional academic essays. The rationale should be written in complete paragraphs.

You may use first person in the rationale. The professional documents should use the voice appropriate to their workplace context.

## Rhetorical Analysis

Each document, the email and memo, required a meticulous arrangement of tone, formatting, and content in order to properly suit the purpose of their audiences.
The memo was directed at leadership staff with the purpose of informing them about a potential scheduling delay, and the email communicated with a coworker to establish a deadline for a specific segment of the upcoming product launch.
These two related but fundamentally different goals dictated the exact combination of tone and content which each document was developed with.
The group nature of informing management suited the broad dispersion of a memo, while the interpersonal communication required to inform the employee best suited the personal structure of an email.

When authoring the email the primary goal was to establish the deadline.
Such an immediate subject required clear and effect communication of the exact requirements and scope of the tasking being assigned.
This included specific details on where to locate the information for the presentation.
The memo also necessitated this urgency, but to a much lesser extent.
Thus the central content of the email focused on providing clear instructions followed by the time in which they must be completed.

In contrast, the memo which only simply needed to inform management of the situation focused primarily on the current condition of the overall product launch, rather then delving down into the specifics required to complete the presentation.
It continued to discuss how the contribution, when eventually finished, would integrate with the launch and lead to a successful event.
This supplementary was only necessary for management and not the employee who was instructed to finish the presentation. 

The overall most challenging aspect of drafting these two documents (other than the creativity required to establish the scenario) was ensuring that each piece of communication remained both formal and respectful while presented as succinctly as possible.
In order to achieve these goals the documents were written with a clear formal tone which lacks common _slang_ or other examples of unprofessional language.
Additionally, ensuring that the email was an efficient call-to-action for the employee while remaining respectful was a particularly difficult challenge.
In industry is is vital that one remains professional and respectful throughout all situations and all employees, even if through unfortunate situations like admonishment.
