# Questions

## Audience and Purpose Questions:
     -Who is the intended audience for the genre?
     -What is the audience likely to know?
     -What is the audience likely to want to know? Why?
     -Why are they reading this document?
     -How much time will this audience want to spend with the information presented?
     -What is the primary purpose of this document?
     -Is there a secondary purpose?
     -How will readers use the information? 
      (Choose a course of action? Buy a product? Complete a task? To gain professional knowledge? To build a professional relationship?)

## Rhetorical Issues:
     -In what settings will this document be used?
     -How does this genre help to establish the information's credibility?
     -How does this genre help to evoke response from the audience?
     -What types of evidence are used to support the claims of the information in the genre?

## Structure:
     -How is the information shaped by the genre? 
      (Consider the limitations/possibilities of space, time, layout, audience, and so on.)
     -How will your document be read? 
     (Beginning to end? Skimmed? Used as a reference? Will different audiences read it in different ways?)
     -How is the genre organized to convey its message?
     -How does the structure facilitate the purpose of the information in the genre?
     -Style/Language: 
     -How formal/informal is the language?
     -What specialize vocabulary is used?
     -What other language features do you notice?
