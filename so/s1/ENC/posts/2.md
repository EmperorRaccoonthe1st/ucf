# Prompt
The article as part of today's reading attempts to show connections but also to stress differences between various terms and definitions of professional and technical communication.
 After reading the review of research and scholarship on the past and future of workplace writing, discuss how at least three ideas / trends / paradigm shifts (discussed in the article and / or from your own internship experiences) impact your personal professional pathway. 

# Response
Language evolves in response to how people's lives change throughout time.
As new trends take hold, or new technologies are utilized, language adapts in order to properly communicate within a professional setting.
During the course of my life a revolution has occurred, a revolution that completely alters how society views written text.
With the advent of _Generative Artificial Intelligence_ a human is no longer required to create text, as it can be written completely autonomously.
This new paradigm will undoubtedly define my future professional pathway, through both how I create text and from where the texts I consume originate from.
It will serve as a tool amplifies my ability to research, analyze, and ideate within my career.

While AI will play an important role within my technical career, understanding and utilizing the foundational rhetorical techniques which have been expertly utilized for millennia is an essential component to communicating effectively throughout my career.
The concept of _ethos, logos, and pathos_ serve as the backbone for any piece of rhetorical writing.
These are the fundamental tools required to build a compelling argument and convey my intent to a coworker or within an organization.
My personal professional pathway will be propelled along by my growth in these core concepts, as I gain more experience I strengthen my abilities to properly utilize them.

Additionally, the concept of _Ethical Rhetoric_ is another integral concept that will define my professional pathway.
Every author, no matter the size of the audience, must take into account the ethical ramifications of their work.
I will eventually author many documents to be utilized within varying organizations, possibly even the public, as these documents affect their audience I must retain and develop my skills of _Ethical Rhetoric_ to ensure there are no harmful effects caused by my work.
